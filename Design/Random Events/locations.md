# Random Events (draft)

Scratch event concepts. Main doc: [Random Events](../Random Events.md).

## Event concepts

### Underground Lake

- **Room:** 3×3 cave with a lake.
- **Workers:** Up to 2 workers can clear the lake from the room.
- **Outcome:** Clearing yields one very large room for the dungeon.
- **Time:** 20 minutes total to clear.
- **XP:** 1 XP per minute, per worker.

### Underground Lake (Gollum's island)

Same as **Underground Lake**, plus:

- **Center tile:** Gollum's island.
- **Gollum:** When revealed, sneaks through the dungeon and can easily kill any worker or minion he finds alone.

### Vein of Ore

- **Worker cap:** Increases by 2.
- **Work:** 2 workers can work the vein.
- **XP:** 1 XP per minute, per worker.

### Dark Cathedral

- **Art:** Environment is in inventory somewhere (path TBD).
- **Hazard:** Option to add ghosts the player must deal with.

### Waterfall entrance

- **Discovery:** Dig uncovers a small 2×1 cave behind a waterfall.
- **Effect:** Hidden entrance for the dungeon.
- **Invaders:** May use this entrance at **¼** the speed of a regular entrance.
