# Start Screen

## Idle state (unpowered core)

On launch: dead silent, deep graphic shadows.

**Nexus core**

- Small unpolished geode at **level-1 core size** (see [General.md](./General.md) "Grid and scale") in the **center cube of the real 3×3×1 core chamber** (`ADungeonShowcaseActor`, same room as [New Game.md](./New%20Game.md))
- **No save:** chamber in plain dark basalt
- **Save exists:** chamber shows the most recent save's biome kit and `CoreColor`—Continue feels like returning home
- Slow continuous spin on vertical axis
- Crystal body: **frosted / milky white quartz** inside rough dark outer rock; very low emission at idle so it reads as pale stone, not hot light—a neutral canvas every glow colour tints clearly
- Faint pulse in crystal fractures: **sapphire** with no save, otherwise the saved `CoreColor`; soft ambient light on the floor tiles
- Bright blooming white is reserved for overload and the New Game flare only; the idle core never reaches it

**Text menu**

- Options: Continue, New, Load, Options
- Crisp translucent sharp-bordered text, left of core—matches in-game tactical HUD layout

## Dynamic hover states (sensing the network)

Cursor over each option sends a small shockwave through the room—a preview of the choice.

### Hover: New (spark of creation)

- Geode locks axis and spins faster
- One bright capillary of liquid light shoots from the core base across the floor to the front wall
- Hit wall blocks hairline-fracture into a faint glowing geometric grid—uncarved canvas before first worker

### Hover: Continue / Load (memory pulse)

- Slow expanding horizontal ring of **pale violet** light sweeps the room, with Will's slow facet shimmer—first lesson that *violet = guidance* (see [Will.md](./Will.md))
- Ring briefly reveals a phantom wireframe dungeon layout on floor and walls; fades after ~1 s

### Hover: Options (calibration)

- Core light shifts from its current pulse colour → stable industrial emerald
- Two satellite crystal shards orbital-lock and spin around the core, reacting to mouse movement

## Click execution (chain reaction)

Instant, un-skippable transition into gameplay or overlay UI—no generic loading screen.

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **New Game** | Camera eases to the setup shot and the card slides in ([New Game.md](./New%20Game.md)). On confirm: core flares white-hot ley-energy; acoustic shatter; front wall fractures to dust; camera pulls through breach into first-room dig UI |
| **Load / Options** | Core drifts back and scales down; stone or crystal slate glides in from the right for saves or settings without breaking the scene |
