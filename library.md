### Haunted Mansion (Fab)

**Type:** Fully modular mansion building kit (environment / architecture)

- Build custom haunted mansions with modular walls, roofs, floors, windows, doors, stairs, columns, and architectural pieces.
- Layouts for interiors, exteriors, gardens, balconies, rooftops, and multi-floor structures.
- Fast iteration for blockout and expansion.
- Fits haunted houses, gothic mansions, abandoned estates, horror maps, and dark cinematic scenes.
- Scale from small houses to large mansions, wings, courtyards, and estate grounds.

**Dungeon Core fit:** Gothic theme surface estate, adventurer approach visuals, or ornate core-adjacent chambers (not 300 UU grid-native without modular retargeting).

## Maintenance

- **Full Fab catalog:** Export or scroll entire Fab library (256+ items) and append to this file, or drop a CSV export into `reference/` and merge.
- **Richer local metadata:** `H:\Unreal Assets\asset-pack-inventory.md` (tags, Gumroad links, folder scans).
- **Do not** commit `.uasset` / `.umap` from these packs into Dungeon Core unless intentionally migrated — reference via external paths or Fab add-to-project.
